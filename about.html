About this dashboard

What the final version supports

A static, reproducible crime analytics dashboard with historical metro rankings, yearly trends, searchable data tables, CSV exports, and district analysis of locally imported real FIR records.

All analytics run in your browser. Charts are bundled locally; an optional OpenStreetMap basemap requires internet access. No account, API key, database, or paid service is needed.

How to interpret the results

  • Metro counts are the team's original transcription, with original source links retained. They have not been independently reverified in this release.
  • District results describe only the imported records and selected filters. Missing years do not automatically mean zero crime.
  • Year-over-year changes need an observed previous calendar year with a nonzero count. Filtered datasets may be incomplete.
  • Demographic and accused/arrested totals report data coverage. Recorded convictions are not equivalent to an official conviction rate.
  • High recorded volume does not establish that a place is more dangerous: population, coverage, and reporting practices vary.

Data-dependent capabilities

Live case tracking and immediate alerts require a connected, maintained case source. Forecasting and patrol optimization require more detailed data and validated models. The supplied project includes neither; this release does not simulate those outcomes or advertise them as operational.
